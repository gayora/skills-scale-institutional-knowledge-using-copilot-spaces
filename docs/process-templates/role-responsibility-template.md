# Role Responsibility Template

Use this template when adding or updating personas in the OctoAcme Roles & Personas document.

- Persona name: (e.g., Release Manager)
- Summary: 1–2 sentence high-level summary of the role
- Responsibilities:
  - Bullet list of 4–8 core responsibilities
- Key interactions:
  - Who to consult (subject-matter experts)
  - Who to inform (stakeholders)
  - Typical handoffs
- Success signals / Acceptance criteria:
  - Observable indicators that this role is fulfilling its responsibilities
- Notes / Related docs:
  - Links to runbooks, incident playbooks, or process docs
